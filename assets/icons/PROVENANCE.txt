Asset provenance

The six *-glass.webp illustrations were created with the built-in image generation tool on October 28, 2026, then resized to 512 pixels and compressed as WebP.
Prompt direction: one front-facing technology emblem in optically clear thick glass, polished silver bevels, white highlights, restrained electric lime reflections, and a charcoal background. Subjects: WordPress circular W, CSS3 shield and numeral 3, HTML5 shield and numeral 5, PHP oval, JavaScript JS tile, and WebP image document.

Small PHP, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript SVG silhouettes originate from Devicon (https://github.com/devicons/devicon), recolored for this portfolio. See DEVICON-LICENSE.txt.
The small WordPress silhouette originates from Simple Icons (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/wordpress.svg), under CC0. WordPress and other technology names and marks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance identifies the technologies used.
The small WebP document glyph is original SVG artwork.
